SILK is a major faction present across the Verse, and holds sway over multiple worlds in Sector 005. A matriarchy, the citizens of SILK allow a benevolent computer to run primary economic and government functions. A direct participatory democracy, citizens engage in local and pan-state referenda and participatory assemblies to deliberate on key SILK policy. The benevolent sentient computer, Loom, takes on the form of a holographic female avatar able to help and aid every citizen individually, whenever they need it. The Divine Web is a steadfast supporter of rights for synthetic intelligences. SILK’s Sector 005 governing body comprises Prime Lucent and SILKendipidity.
