The Alarian Star Empire, also known as the Alarian Imperium, is a brutal, militaristic empire. Founded by the divine Empress Alaria, subjects of Alaria deify their fallen leaders and welcome each Empress to an ever growing pantheon of worship. Through great martial deeds and service to the state subjects of the Imperium can gain citizenship and voting rights, joining the ranks of the Elect. Alarians pride themselves on their martial prowess, skill in combat, lethal and dangerous sports - often proven through large scale gladiatorial games - veteran and honoured warriors are granted the rank of Children within the Alarian Imperium, a supreme rank providing access to and forming the court followers of the Empress Alaria VI. The Alarian Star Empire was forged through war and conquest by Empress Alaria out of the wreck of the Earth Empire on the world of Alioth and its surrounding systems. Through three decades of brutal crusade and atomic fire, Alaria and her Children built a new empire from the old. The colonies of the Imperium in Sector 005 are governed by First Child Lucien Ashborn.
