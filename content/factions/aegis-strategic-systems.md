**Headquarters:** United Terran Federation

**Sector:** Military–Industrial Complex

**Nickname:** “The Arsenal”

Controls nearly the entire UTF war machine.

**Subsidiaries include:**

- Aegis Fleetworks – Warship construction
- Helios Ordnance – Weapons and ammunition
- Titan Combat Systems – Power armor and infantry gear
- Proxima Tactical Logistics – Military supply chains
- Legion Training Group – Officer academies and combat training
- Victory Assurance – Veteran pensions and death benefits

**Influence:**

If Aegis stopped operating for one week, the UTF Navy would collapse.

They maintain close relationships with the Federal Navy Admiralty.

**Corporate power bloc:** Expansion Bloc
