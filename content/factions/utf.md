The United Terran Federation is the rump successor state to the old Earth Empire. The Federation maintains a significant influence over galactic politics as the ancestral home of Human civilisation, controlling several key Core systems and planets across all colonial sectors. The Terran Federation is a militarised and autocratic oligarchy, maintaining the pretence of democratic institutional features and the largest armed forces in human space. The Terran Federation seeks to regain its lost colonial holdings across settled human space, by any means necessary. The current Supreme Chancellor is Mohammed El-Fridi, and Sector 005 Governor is Prem Sheev and Director Robert Sykes-Picot. Service Guarantees Citizenship!
