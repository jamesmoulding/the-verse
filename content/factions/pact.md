The Pact is the collective defence alliance of democratic polities founded by the [Proximan Articles of Coalition Treaty](/diplomacy/treaties/proximan-articles-of-coalition/), signed at the Hall of the Republic on Proxima in 2533. Led by the Proxima Federal Republic, it binds together a buffer region of worlds and independent polities between the United Terran Federation and the PFR, chiefly across Sector 002 and Sector 003, along with members further afield. It is Proxima's main instrument for limiting Terran expansion into the Colonial Sectors.

## Members

- [Proxima Federal Republic](/factions/pfr/), the leading power and depositary of the treaty
- [Queeg Heptarchy](/factions/queeg-heptarchy/) (Sector 002), a Proximan protectorate and buffer state on the Terran border
- [Dyrektoriat Kosmiczny](/factions/dyrektoriat-kosmiczny/) (Sector 003)
- [Outer Rim Alliance](/factions/outer-rim-alliance/) (Sectors 003 and 004)

- [New Star Coalition](/factions/new-star-coalition/) (Sectors 004 and 003)

## How the Pact works

The members undertake to settle their disputes peacefully, to strengthen their free institutions and economic collaboration, and to build their individual and collective capacity to resist attack. The heart of the treaty is Article V: an armed attack on any member, anywhere in settled human space, is treated as an attack on them all, and each member will assist those attacked with whatever action it deems necessary, including armed force. Article VI extends that guarantee to members' colonial holdings, orbital installations, vessels and forces wherever they are stationed.

The **Pact Council**, made up of representatives of every member, meets whenever a member believes its territory, independence or security is threatened. New states may join only by unanimous agreement of the existing members, depositing their accession with the Proximan Federal Government. The treaty runs indefinitely; after twenty years any member may leave on a year's notice.

The Pact signed the [Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War](/diplomacy/treaties/interstellar-covenant/) on behalf of all its members in 2533, and its [Emergency Amendment](/diplomacy/treaties/emergency-amendment/), the Bionid Exemption, in 2582.

## The Pact in Sector 005 news

**The Hobus crisis (Star Year 2600).** After a series of explosions on the Terran world of Hobus killed thousands, the Terran Federation blamed the neighbouring Queeg and put its Fourth Battlegroup on maximum readiness. In the Uzzayad, the Queeg Parliament, Qaliph Hawiya Gentlos IV called for the Pact to be activated. Prime Minister Svetlana Johnsdottir declared that "Proxima will stand by the Queeg", while the National Vanguard Movement demanded "fleets, factories, and citizens ready to defend the Pact" and peace protests spread across the Republic. The Uzzayad voted against activating the Pact, hoping to placate its protesting border worlds, but asked for support from allied forces.

When Queeg border worlds then invited "Independent Quagaar Auxiliaries" into their systems, formations that Pact intelligence sources described as reflagged Terran units, the Uzzayad called an emergency session of the Pact Council and Proxima sent its Second Battlegroup into Sector 002 as a guarantee of Queeg territorial integrity. Pact hardliners welcomed the move; markets and moderates saw a sharply higher risk of open confrontation. On Starkiller Day, Johnsdottir reaffirmed Proxima's commitment to the Pact before a special session of the Federal Parliament.

**Pressure within the coalition (Star Year 2602).** Commentators noted that Johnsdottir faces pressure from the wider Pact coalition to be seen as a friend and a builder, not only as Terra's cold war rival.

**The Amity Station atrocity (Star Year 2602).** At an emergency session attended by all the major signatory states, the Pact Council voted unanimously to call for a new interstellar criminal court. Members spoke out against the Alarian Star Empire, arguing that the actions of its Sector 005 colonial government amount to a war crime that must be investigated. The Council also called for an end to hostilities between the Skaro Pact and the LGR. Untar Fentzeen, Lord President of the Union of Prax, thanked the PFR's colonial officials in Sector 005 for their restraint and diplomatic leadership.
