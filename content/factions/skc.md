The Seekers are both religious, maltheist and expansionist - they see it as their sacred duty to spit in the eye of the maker and all of this vast blackness by making sure as many Seekers survive as possible, and through leaving behind things, like glorious churches. We can’t take it with us after all, say the Seekers in their holy text. Seekers are meticulous in their record keeping, every ship, station, port and settlement maintains a log of everyone who has been there. Whilst not particularly warlike, the Seekers aim to make their mark on the great blackness and have expanded significantly from their beginnings aboard the First Station. It was there, on Revelation Station as it is now known, an abandoned corporate mining asteroid, where the first teachings were scribed, as the miners, abandoned, with no food, air recyclers or transport, were left to die - the first Seekers. 100 years after the atrocity carried out by the Zeyland-Butani Corporation, though they deny it, the Seekers Commonwealth spans multiple colonial sectors. The Seekers Commonwealth is governed by Governor Dæmene Jarod Bilder.
