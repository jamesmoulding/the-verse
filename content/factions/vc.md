The worlds of the Viśvanivāsaḥ Confederacy are thriving pilgrimage sites, millions of Brydists make the trip from across human settled space to pay their respects to the Order of the Golden Lion and their founder John Bryden. The teachings of the Golden Lion, a living document known as the Book of G’Kar, span more than 500 years of wisdom and are held on public display on Earthsea. The Brydist annals plot the beginnings of the Order to the two founders, John Bryden and James Moulding, two British spiritual pioneers, who invested their savings, known as the Golden Pledge, which over the years enabled their followers in 2375 to launch the Great Pilgrimage which founded the colony world of Earthsea in the relatively unexplored Sector 005 on the planet VX71OP1.0084. The Viśvanivāsaḥ Confederacy is facilitated by the Roke Nexus and represented by The Uhura, appointed by the community of the Golden Lion.
