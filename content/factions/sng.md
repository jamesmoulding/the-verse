SNG is an alliance between the Solas Na Gealai and the people of E’lyra. E’lyra was a decentralised, spiritually connected nomadic group forced from their world by corporate exploitation. With their world stripped bare, they fled to the stars seeking a new home. When the E’lyran fleet appeared on the edges of the Luna Piena system, the inhabitants of the system, the Solas Na Gealai and the nomads both feared a conflict - instead, the people of Luna Piena offered them sanctuary and something greater, cohabitation and mutual governance. This alliance birthed a new hybrid civilization, with Luna Piena as the heart of a system built on healing, autonomy, and mutual care. The peoples of the SNG are governed by Tellan Leisval.
