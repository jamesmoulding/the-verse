The year is 2600. It is 110 years since the fall of the old United Earth Empire, and a decade since the end of the catastrophic Bionid War.

The Verse takes place in Sector 005, encompassing a star cluster distant from many of the capital worlds of factions present in Sector 005. Several factions have established developed colony worlds in the Sector and seek to exploit and consolidate their positions among the stars.
