The “Twelve Directorships” collectively control ~98% of the UTF economy. Each megacorp is actually a holding structure containing thousands of subsidiaries, shell companies, and sector monopolies. The boards of these twelve corporations form the Strategic Economic Council, the body that truly determines who becomes Supreme Chancellor.

### 1. Aegis Strategic Systems

**Sector:** Military–Industrial Complex

**Nickname:** “The Arsenal”

Controls nearly the entire UTF war machine.

**Subsidiaries include:**

- Aegis Fleetworks – Warship construction
- Helios Ordnance – Weapons and ammunition
- Titan Combat Systems – Power armor and infantry gear
- Proxima Tactical Logistics – Military supply chains
- Legion Training Group – Officer academies and combat training
- Victory Assurance – Veteran pensions and death benefits

**Influence:**

If Aegis stopped operating for one week, the UTF Navy would collapse.

They maintain close relationships with the Federal Navy Admiralty.

## The Real Government

### The Strategic Economic Council

The twelve megacorps each appoint one Director-General.

These twelve individuals form the Strategic Economic Council (SEC).

**Officially:**

“An advisory body representing the productive forces of the Federation.”

**In reality:**

**They decide:**

- Supreme Chancellor candidates
- military budgets
- colonial policy
- interstellar trade strategy

## Political Theatre

The Supreme Chancellor is selected through a managed process.

- Step 1: Corporate vetting
- Step 2: Internal party manoeuvring
- Step 3: Controlled election

The population believes they are choosing between political factions.

In reality they are choosing between corporate coalitions.

## Corporate Power Blocs

The Twelve split into three major factions.

### Expansion Bloc

Aggressive reconquest of lost colonies.

- Aegis Strategic Systems
- Atlas Stellar Extraction
- Horizon Logistics
- Sentinel Security

### Stability Bloc

Maintain order and profitability.

- Aurelius Financial
- Civitas Systems
- VoxCom Media
- NovaLearn Education

### Prosperity Bloc

Focus on growth and infrastructure.

- SolDyne Energy
- Orbis Infrastructure
- OmniFood
- Helix BioSolutions

## How Citizens Experience This

Most citizens never hear the megacorp names.

Instead, they interact with friendly brands:

- HelixMed Hospitals
- NovaLearn Academies
- Horizon Freight
- Sentinel Civic Protection

**The illusion:**

The Federation provides everything.

**The truth:**

Twelve corporations own civilisation.
