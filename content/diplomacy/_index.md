## Major Treaties

Treaties established and governing human settled space

- [AI Prohibition Instruments](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kur7Qcm8oW7j2BgkITlyhDf0VgIox5UL/edit?usp=sharing) (United Nations, United Earth Republic) 2350
  - Translated into domestic law via Earth Empire, applies to UTF, PFR, ASE
- [Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ghM_mWGI1a27N0WwXMUvjNoN0i165-Gv6fCvxJ_FXkY/edit?usp=sharing) 2532
  - Applies to PFR, SKC, SNG, VCW, SILK, VC, PACT
- [Proximan Articles of Coalition Treaty](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OA4_GwfgET5ndWH2k4FO92CTL-dfGjuDYQnuRGk20us/edit?usp=sharing) (PACT) 2533
- [Emergency Amendment to the Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xl_NAgcSJLrIhh3f_5Pn-oBBD3uIgk8LI02T02CHT6g/edit?usp=sharing) 2582
  - Applies to PFR, PACT, SKC, SNG, VCW, SILK, VC, CPA, AFCO
- New Concordat 2588
  - Signatory states failed to domestically ratify significant portions of the overall treaty. However, treaty stipulations accepted by all major powers and successfully translated into domestic law including **new restrictions on nuclear weapons, limiting their use to ship to ship combat and use against Bionid threats**, superseding the Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War on such matters.
