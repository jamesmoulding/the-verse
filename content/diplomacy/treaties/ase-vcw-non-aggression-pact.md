AI and VCW, hereafter referred to as "the Parties", enter into this Non-Aggression Pact to ensure stability, prevent unnecessary conflict and maintain strategic balance between their respective spheres of influence. This agreement is founded on mutual recognition of sovereignty and the shared interest in avoiding open hostilities.

## Article I: Non-aggression commitment

1. The Parties agree to refrain from all forms of direct military aggression against one another.
2. Prohibited actions include armed invasion; orbital strikes or planetary bombardment; cyberwarfare intended to cause systemic disruption or collapse; and use of proxy forces, mercenaries or deniable assets.

## Article II: Territorial integrity

1. Each Party shall respect the existing territorial holdings of the other.
2. No attempts shall be made to annex, destabilise or covertly undermine control of systems belonging to the opposing Party.
3. Any disputes over territory shall be resolved exclusively through diplomatic negotiation.

## Article III: Non-interference

1. The Parties shall not interfere in the internal governance, succession or political systems of the other.
2. This includes, but is not limited to, supporting rebellions or insurgent movements; espionage aimed at destabilisation; and economic sabotage or coercive manipulation.

## Article IV: Military posture

1. Both Parties agree to avoid provocative force build-ups in border-adjacent regions.
2. Large-scale military exercises near the other Party's territory must be preceded by reasonable notification.

## Article V: Communication protocol

1. A secure direct communication channel shall be maintained between AI and VCW command structures.
2. This channel shall be used for crisis de-escalation, clarification of intent and prevention of miscalculation.

## Article VI: Third-party conflicts

1. Neither Party is obliged to support the other in external conflicts.
2. However, neither Party shall provide assistance to entities actively engaged in warfare against the other.

## Article VII: Trade and cooperation

1. The Parties may engage in trade, scientific exchange or limited cooperation where mutually beneficial.
2. Such cooperation shall not compromise security, sovereignty or strategic autonomy.

## Article VIII: Duration and review

1. This Pact shall remain in effect for five (5) standard years from the date of signing.
2. Renewal, amendment or termination may occur by mutual consent.
3. A formal review shall take place at the midpoint of the term.

## Article IX: Withdrawal

1. Either Party may withdraw from this Pact with six (6) months' written notice.
2. Immediate termination is permitted only in the event of a verified material breach.

## Article X: Breach of agreement

1. Any violation shall trigger immediate diplomatic consultation.
2. Repeated or severe breaches may result in suspension or termination of the Pact and the restoration of unrestricted strategic posture.

## Conclusion

This agreement reflects a shared understanding that restraint is preferable to escalation, and that coexistence, however conditional, is preferable to open conflict. AI and VCW affirm this Pact as a framework for controlled stability over its five-year term.

*Signed: AI, VCW. Date: 2601, Turn 8.*
