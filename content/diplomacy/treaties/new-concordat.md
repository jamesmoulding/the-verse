*From* A Potted History of Advanced Artificial Intelligence, *the Paladin Long Read Series.*

The New Concordat of 2588 was proposed in the wake of the Bionid War by the Proxima Federal Republic as a desperate measure of civilisational solidarity. It aimed to build on the collective efforts of the Supreme Allied Fleet and establish common policies on key areas, including WMD proliferation, WMD and nuclear use, AI prohibition and Bionid monitoring.

After a week of high-level talks, a common text was signed by all of the major powers present. Following the talks, however, the polities cherry-picked which sections they ratified.

## What was ratified

AI prohibition was largely ignored during the ratification efforts of non-prohibiting states. But the New Concordat established the first universal declaration and policy on WMD and nuclear use. The stipulations accepted by all major powers and translated into domestic law include **new restrictions on nuclear weapons, limiting their use to ship-to-ship combat and use against Bionid threats**, superseding the Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War on such matters.

## Why AI prohibition failed

The failure on AI prohibition is structural. SILK, CASI, the new LGR and NSSF, and the Viśvanivāsaḥ Confederacy cannot sign any treaty that would dissolve their societies. The Alarian Imperium will not sign any treaty that would deprive it of a casus belli. The PFR and the UTF will not sign any treaty that would grant legitimacy to what they regard as the ongoing, systemic violation of the Harper Directive. The Cerean Planetary Alliance, cautious of AI and running its own domestic monitoring programmes, has signed several treaties and honoured none of them. The Seekers regard the question as theological and a risk to their business ventures, and have not engaged actively on it.
