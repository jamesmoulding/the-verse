*Adopted by the Interstellar Council of the Nations (ICON), Emergency Session, Proxima, 2582. Adopted in extraordinary session pursuant to Article 15 of the Covenant.*

## Preamble

The undersigned nations, convened in extraordinary emergency session of the Interstellar Council of the Nations at Proxima in the year 2582, having received and verified reports of the advance of the Bionid hivemind across colonial space and the total consumption of inhabited worlds in its path,

Acknowledging that the Bionid entity constitutes a threat of an unprecedented and existential nature, one that admits no negotiation, no surrender and no distinction between combatant and civilian, and that in its advance it has itself rendered moot the protections this Covenant was designed to uphold,

Recognising that conventional military doctrine and the restrictions of Article 9 of the Covenant, designed to protect civilian populations from the actions of signatory and non-signatory nations alike, were not conceived with the existence of a non-sentient, non-sovereign biological hive entity in mind,

Affirming that this Amendment constitutes a narrow and time-limited emergency measure, that the underlying principles of the Covenant remain fully in force, and that this exemption shall not be interpreted as weakening those principles in any conflict between nations,

Do hereby adopt the following amendment to the Interstellar Covenant on the Conduct of War.

## Amendment I

### Article 9A: Exemption for Bionid combat operations

Notwithstanding the prohibitions established under Article 9 of this Covenant, the use of thermonuclear bombardment and other weapons otherwise prohibited under that Article is hereby authorised under the following strictly defined conditions:

1. The target of such weapons must be confirmed Bionid biomass, Bionid fleet assets, or planetary surfaces confirmed to be under active Bionid infestation with no recoverable civilian population remaining.
2. Before any thermonuclear or equivalent bombardment of an inhabited or previously inhabited world, the commanding authority must certify in writing that all reasonable efforts to evacuate surviving civilian populations have been made or have been rendered impossible by the nature and speed of the Bionid advance.
3. The use of biological agents, toxic atmospheric dispersal or any other prohibited method remains forbidden in all circumstances, including against Bionid targets, where such use would pose a material risk of harm to surviving human populations in adjacent systems.
4. This exemption extends only to armed forces operating under the command of the Supreme Allied Fleet and to the military forces of signatory and participating nations acting in co-ordination with it. It does not constitute a general licence for any individual nation to conduct independent operations using prohibited weapons outside that command structure without the approval of a majority of signatory nations.
5. A record of every instance in which weapons covered by this exemption are deployed shall be filed with the ICON Secretariat within thirty standard days of deployment, including the certifications required under clause 2.

### Article 9B: Sunset and review

This Amendment shall remain in force only for a period of twenty stellar years or the duration of active hostilities against the Bionid entity. Upon the cessation of those hostilities, the exemption provided under Article 9A shall lapse automatically and Article 9 of the Covenant shall resume full effect without the need for further amendment. The ICON Council shall convene within one standard year of the end of hostilities to review all deployments made under this exemption and to consider whether any further action is required.

### Article 9C: Non-precedent

Nothing in this Amendment shall be construed as establishing a precedent for the suspension or modification of Article 9 or any other article of the Covenant in any future conflict between nations. The circumstances giving rise to this Amendment are recognised as singular and shall not be cited in justification of the use of prohibited weapons in any inter-nation dispute.

## Signatories

*Signed at Vertinia, in the year 2582.* The Cerean Planetary Alliance and the Association of Free Colonies signed in association, not constituting full accession to the Covenant.

This Amendment has not been signed by the United Terran Federation, Association of Free Colonies or the Alarian Imperium, who declined to participate in the Emergency Session. The Supreme Allied Fleet nevertheless called upon the forces of all nations engaged in Bionid combat operations to observe the conditions set out in Article 9A as a matter of interstellar conscience, regardless of their signatory status.
