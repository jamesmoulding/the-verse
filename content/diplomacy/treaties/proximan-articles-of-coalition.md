*Signed at the Hall of the Republic, Proxima, Standard Year 2533.*

## Preamble

The nations hereby party to this Treaty, reaffirming their commitment to the purposes and principles of interstellar peace and collective security, resolved to unite their efforts for collective defence and the preservation of peace and security across settled human space, and recognising that the security of free peoples is indivisible across sectors and systems, agree to this Treaty.

## Articles

**Article I.** The parties undertake to settle any international disputes in which they may be involved by peaceful means in such a manner that interstellar peace, security and justice are not endangered, and to refrain in their international relations from the threat or use of force in any manner inconsistent with the maintenance of peace across settled human space.

**Article II.** The parties will contribute toward the further development of peaceful and friendly interstellar relations by strengthening their free institutions, by bringing about a better understanding of the principles upon which those institutions are founded, and by promoting conditions of stability and well-being. They will seek to eliminate conflict in their interstellar economic policies and will encourage economic collaboration between any or all of them.

**Article III.** In order more effectively to achieve the objectives of this Treaty, the parties, separately and jointly, by means of continuous and effective self-help and mutual aid, will maintain and develop their individual and collective capacity to resist armed attack.

**Article IV.** The parties will consult together whenever, in the opinion of any of them, the territorial integrity, political independence or security of any of the parties is threatened. The Pact Council, composed of representatives of all parties, shall be constituted to consider matters concerning the implementation of this Treaty and shall meet as circumstances require.

**Article V.** The parties agree that an armed attack against one or more of them in any sector of settled human space shall be considered an attack against them all, and consequently they agree that, if such an armed attack occurs, each of them, in exercise of the right of individual or collective self-defence, will assist the party or parties so attacked by taking such action as it deems necessary, including the use of armed force, to restore and maintain the security of the coalition.

**Article VI.** For the purpose of Article V, an armed attack shall be deemed to include an armed attack on the territory, colonial holdings, orbital installations, vessels or military forces of any party, wherever stationed in settled human space.

**Article VII.** This Treaty does not affect, and shall not be interpreted as affecting, in any way the existing rights and obligations of the parties under any prior interstellar agreements to which they are signatories, or their responsibility to pursue the maintenance of interstellar peace and security through peaceful means wherever possible.

**Article VIII.** Any state sharing the principles of this Treaty and in a position to contribute to the security of settled human space may be invited to accede to this Treaty by unanimous agreement of the existing parties. Accession shall take effect upon deposit of the instrument of accession with the Proximan Federal Government, which is hereby designated the depositary of this Treaty.

**Article IX.** This Treaty shall be of indefinite duration. After it has been in force for twenty standard years, any party may cease to be a party by giving one standard year's notice of its denunciation to the Proximan Federal Government, which shall inform the other parties of the deposit of each such instrument of denunciation.

*Deposited with the Proximan Federal Government, Hall of the Republic, Proxima, Standard Year 2533.*
