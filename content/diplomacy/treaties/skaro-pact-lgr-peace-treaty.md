In order to bring an end to hostilities and establish a durable peace, the parties agree to the following terms.

## Article I: Recognition of Rikarian independence

The future of Riker II will be determined by its people. A referendum will be held so that the colonists can choose which polity, if any, they wish to join. This will be held within 6 months of the ratification of this treaty. Any polity may send an observer to ensure this process proceeds fairly and without incident, with any complaints adjudicated by a cross-polity judicial committee. LGR will attempt to contact formerly-aligned resistance forces and seek to end the civil conflict on the planet.

## Article II: Cessation of hostilities

All parties shall immediately cease offensive military operations against the other. The Skaro Pact (UTF, ASE) shall immediately withdraw all military forces from the NW Scouting Sector.

## Article III: Restoration of sovereignty

The Skaro Pact shall relinquish any and all claims enforced by military action to LGR or independent worlds.

## Article IV: Release of prisoners

The Skaro Pact will release any and all LGR prisoners of war.
