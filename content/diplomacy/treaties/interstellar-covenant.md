*Adopted by the Interstellar Council of the Nations (ICON), Proxima, 2532.*

## Preamble

The undersigned nations, convened under the auspices of the Interstellar Council of the Nations at Proxima in the stellar year 2532, having witnessed with horror the mass civilian casualties inflicted across settled human space during the Alarian Wars of Expansion, and reaffirming the principles of dignity, mercy and restraint that have guided the laws of war since the earliest centuries of human civilisation,

Recognising that the conduct of armed conflict between interstellar nations carries consequences of unprecedented scale across inhabited worlds, orbital habitats, colony stations and the civilian populations therein,

Resolved that no military or political objective may justify the deliberate destruction of civilian life or the abandonment of basic obligations toward the wounded, the captured and the defenceless,

Do hereby adopt and commit to uphold the following articles as the minimum standard of conduct binding upon all signatory nations, their armed forces, their contracted military agents and all persons acting under their authority in time of armed conflict.

## Part I: Protection of civilians

**Article 1: Distinction.** Parties to any armed conflict shall at all times distinguish between civilian persons and combatants, and between civilian objects and military objectives. Attacks shall be directed only against military objectives. Indiscriminate bombardment of inhabited worlds, colony settlements, orbital habitats or population centres is prohibited.

**Article 2: Proportionality.** An attack is prohibited where it may be expected to cause civilian casualties or damage to civilian objects that would be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated.

**Article 3: Precaution.** All feasible precautions shall be taken to avoid and minimise civilian casualties. Warning shall be given of attacks likely to affect civilian populations, unless the circumstances do not permit it.

**Article 4: Protected persons.** Civilian persons who take no direct part in hostilities shall be protected against violence to their life and person, including murder, torture, mutilation and cruel treatment of any kind, without exception.

**Article 5: Starvation and blockade.** The deliberate starvation of civilian populations as a method of warfare is prohibited. Blockades shall not prevent the passage of humanitarian relief supplies to civilian populations in need.

## Part II: Treatment of prisoners and the wounded

**Article 6: Wounded and sick.** All wounded and sick combatants, regardless of the party to which they belong, shall be respected and protected. Medical personnel, facilities and vessels engaged solely in the treatment of the wounded or sick shall not be attacked.

**Article 7: Prisoners of war.** Persons who have laid down their arms or are otherwise rendered hors de combat shall be treated humanely and without adverse distinction. They shall not be subjected to torture, murder or degrading treatment, and shall be entitled to communicate their status to their families and to a neutral authority.

**Article 8: Internment.** Prisoners of war and civilian internees shall receive adequate food, shelter and medical care. Their detention shall not constitute punishment, and they shall be released and repatriated without delay once active hostilities have ceased.

## Part III: Prohibited methods and means of warfare

**Article 9: Weapons of mass destruction.** The use of weapons designed to cause indiscriminate mass casualties across civilian populations, including but not limited to thermonuclear bombardment, biological agents, toxic atmospheric dispersal and mass-driver strikes upon inhabited worlds, is prohibited.

**Article 10: Environmental destruction.** The deliberate destruction of a planet's environment, atmosphere or ecological systems on a scale likely to cause long-term harm to civilian life is prohibited.

**Article 11: No quarter.** It is prohibited to order that there shall be no survivors, to threaten an adversary therewith, or to conduct hostilities on that basis.

## Part IV: Application and oversight

**Article 12: Universal application.** These articles apply to all armed conflicts between signatory nations, and to conflicts between a signatory nation and a non-signatory nation where the latter has declared or demonstrated acceptance of these obligations.

**Article 13: Non-derogation.** No military necessity, political circumstance or order from any commanding authority may be invoked to justify a breach of these articles. Individuals who commit, order or knowingly facilitate violations may be held personally responsible.

**Article 14: Neutral oversight.** Signatory nations shall permit access by agreed neutral observers or ICON-designated monitors to prisoner facilities and conflict zones, subject to reasonable military security requirements.

**Article 15: Amendment.** This Covenant may be amended only by agreement of a two-thirds majority of signatory nations meeting in full session of the Interstellar Council of the Nations.

## Signatories

*Signed at the Hall of the Republic, Proxima, in the year 2532.*

This Covenant has not been signed by the United Terran Federation, the Alarian Star Empire, the Association of Free Colonies, the Union of Prax, the Cerean Planetary Alliance, or any other polity not named. The Interstellar Council of the Nations calls upon all nations of settled human space to affirm these principles without delay.
