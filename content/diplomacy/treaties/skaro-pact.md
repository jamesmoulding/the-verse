The contracting parties, reaffirming their desire for the organisation of a system of collective security in Sector 005, to be known henceforth as the Skaro Pact Organisation (SPO),

Taking into consideration that the situation obtaining in Sector 005 as the result of Bionid invasions and a history of violence and instability is a menace to the security of the peace-loving polities, and that under these circumstances the peace-loving polities of Sector 005 will take the necessary measures for safeguarding their security in the interests of maintaining peace in the Sector,

In the interests of further strengthening and promoting friendship, co-operation and mutual assistance, in accordance with the principles of respect for the independence and sovereignty of polities and of non-interference in their internal affairs, have resolved to conclude this Treaty of Friendship, Co-operation and Mutual Assistance.

**Article 1.** The Founding Members of the Skaro Pact Organisation (SPO) shall be (a) the United Terran Federation and (b) the Alarian Imperium.

**Article 2.** The contracting parties declare their readiness to take part, in the spirit of sincere co-operation, in all system-wide undertakings intended to safeguard peace and security, and they shall use all their energies for the realisation of these aims.

**Article 3.** The contracting parties shall take council among themselves on all important system-wide questions relating to their common interests, guided by the interests of strengthening system-wide peace and security. They shall take council among themselves immediately whenever, in the opinion of any of them, there has arisen the threat of an armed attack on one or several polities that are signatories of the treaty, in the interests of organising their joint defence and of upholding peace and security.

**Article 4.** In the event of an armed attack in Sector 005 on one or several polities that are signatories of the treaty, each state that is a party to this treaty shall, in the exercise of the right to individual or collective self-defence, render the polity or polities so attacked immediate assistance, individually and in agreement with other polities that are parties to this treaty, by all the means it may consider necessary, including the use of armed force. The polities that are parties to this treaty shall immediately take council among themselves concerning the necessary joint measures to be adopted for the purpose of restoring and upholding sector peace and security.

**Article 5.** The contracting parties undertake not to participate in any coalitions and alliances, and not to conclude any agreements, the purposes of which would be at variance with those of the present treaty.

**Article 6.** The contracting parties declare that they will act in the spirit of friendship and co-operation with the object of furthering the development of, and strengthening, the economic and cultural relations between them, adhering to the principles of mutual respect for their independence and sovereignty, and of non-interference in their internal affairs.

**Article 7.** The present treaty is open to be acceded to by other polities, irrespective of their social and political systems, which may express their readiness to assist, through participation in the present treaty, in combining the efforts of the peace-loving polities for the purpose of safeguarding the peace and security of Sector 005. This act of acceding to the treaty shall become effective with the unanimous consent of all polities that are parties to this treaty.

**Article 8.** The SPO will establish a headquarters on the planet Skaro (which is recognised as the sovereign territory of the Alarian Imperium) for the purpose of coordinating peaceful and thriving cooperation in Sector 005, and all signatories of the pact will be granted access to the Skaro artifact (recognised as the collective property of the SPO).

**Article 50.** The present treaty shall remain in force for 20 years. For the contracting parties which have not submitted to the Skaro Headquarters a statement denouncing the treaty a year before the expiration of its term, it shall remain in force throughout the following 10 years.
