## Preamble

Recognising the mutual interest of fostering prosperity, security and orderly commerce within Sector Five, the Vertinia Commonwealth (VCW) and Solas Na Gealaí (SNG) hereby establish this Free Trade Agreement. Both parties affirm their commitment to peaceful exchange, stable trade networks and coordinated operations within the sector.

## Article I: Freedom of commerce

1. Merchants, vessels and trade envoys of both states shall be granted full rights to move, buy and sell goods within designated trade zones of Sector Five, with the exception of the goods and services outlined in Appendix I.
2. No tariffs, duties or levies shall be imposed on trade between the two signatories, except as mutually agreed in later amendments.

## Article II: Protection of trade routes

1. Both powers shall cooperate to ensure the safety and continuity of trade convoys within Sector Five.
2. Acts of piracy, sabotage, espionage or unlawful interference with trade shall be treated as crimes subject to investigation and enforcement under the agreed mechanisms of this Treaty.
3. For the purposes of maintaining uninterrupted operations, signatories may deploy patrol squadrons along established trade routes, within their jurisdiction, to ensure safe passage.
4. Both parties shall ensure that critical trade and logistics hubs operate in a manner that endeavours to achieve uninterrupted commerce, coordinated to maintain sector stability.
5. Measures necessary to maintain the security and integrity of trade operations may be implemented in accordance with guidance from both signatories of the Treaty, ensuring efficiency and sector-wide stability.

## Article III: Resolution of disputes

1. Disputes arising under this agreement shall be referred to a neutral arbitration council composed of representatives from both sides.
2. The arbitration council shall be chaired by an agreed-upon chair, whose role shall be to facilitate orderly and impartial proceedings.
3. Decisions of the arbitration council shall be guided by both signatories of the Treaty to ensure consistent and effective resolution.
4. Should either signatory find they are fundamentally opposed to the ruling of the arbitration council, they reserve the right to begin proceedings to dissolve this treaty in a peaceful manner.

## Article IV: Exchange of strategic resource

1. Both parties agree to review trade of strategic resources at regular intervals, and endeavour to establish said trade routes, upon completion of the necessary infrastructure to facilitate such trades, if desired by both parties.
2. The exchange shall be conducted under mutually agreed procedures designed to ensure continuity, stability and equitable benefit to both parties.
3. Both parties shall consult and coordinate regarding scheduling, logistics and safeguarding of the resource during the exchange process.
4. Both parties are free to establish similar strategic trade deals with parties not included within this treaty.

## Article V: Non-aggression pact

1. Both parties agree to refrain from initiating any hostile actions, military incursions or coercive measures against one another within Sector Five and adjacent territories.
2. Any disputes or disagreements shall be resolved exclusively through diplomatic channels or the arbitration council outlined in Article III.

## Article VI: Dissolution of the agreement

Both parties reserve the right to peacefully and unilaterally dissolve this agreement under the following terms:

1. The initiating party must provide a formal dissolution request in writing to the other party.
2. The dissolution process allows a period of 100 days for both parties to conclude trade and diplomatic operations under this agreement.
3. The initiating party reserves the right to withdraw the dissolution request during this period.
4. If withdrawn, the initiating party cannot submit another dissolution request for an additional 100 days.

*In witness whereof, the undersigned, duly authorised by their respective governments, have signed this Treaty and affixed their seals.*

For the Vertinia Commonwealth: **Chief Aylen Winterglow** and **Exarch Nina Silverleaf**.

For Solas Na Gealaí: **Fleet Admiral Tellan Liesval E'lyran**, Dyad of Solas Na Gealaí.

## Appendix I: Prohibited goods and services

Within the sovereign domains of the Vertinia Commonwealth and of Solas Na Gealaí, an extensive list of prohibited goods, such as harmful drugs, and services, such as human trafficking, as fitting a modern 27th-century economy.
