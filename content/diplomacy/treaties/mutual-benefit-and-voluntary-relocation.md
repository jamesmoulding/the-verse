## Article I: Voluntary emigration from SILK

1. Any recognised member of SILK who expresses a sustained preference not to exist under machine governance, and who meets the eligibility standards established under this Treaty, may request voluntary departure.
2. Such requests shall be made through an individual application process administered by SILK. Eligibility requires (a) individually expressed interest, (b) individual submission to the Central Intelligence Core, and (c) confirmation that the request was formed under ordinary civic conditions.
3. SILK shall permit such individuals to depart without obstruction, delay or post-departure obligation, provided all procedural requirements are satisfied.
4. Upon departure, such individuals shall be received by the Alarian Imperium.

## Article II: Imperial Resettlement Authority

1. Individuals departing SILK under this Treaty shall be relocated to the Celestalis System.
2. Upon resettlement, individuals shall fall solely under Imperial jurisdiction and protection.

## Article III: Applications for relocation to SILK

1. Residents of the Alarian Imperium who express interest in relocation to SILK may submit applications to the Imperial Government.
2. The Imperium shall yearly transmit a list of applicants to SILK for consideration.

## Article IV: Information

Only information strictly necessary for the administration of relocations under this Treaty shall be exchanged.

## Article V: Selection autonomy

1. Relocation under this Treaty shall not be subject to numerical parity or proportional exchange.
2. The Imperium retains full authority to screen applications for relocation to SILK.

## Article VI: Commencement

This Treaty shall enter into force at the beginning of the year 2603.

## Article VII: Duration and review

1. This Treaty shall remain in force indefinitely.
2. Reviews may be conducted periodically.
3. Amendments require ratification by both Parties.

## Article VIII: Exceptional provisions

During periods of extraordinary civic disruption, SILK may pause application intake or adjudication until ordinary conditions resume. Such deferral shall be procedural, a date shall be established for review, and it shall not constitute obstruction under this Treaty.

## Signatories

For the Alarian Imperium: **Lucian Ashborn**, Authorised Representative of the Central Imperial Government.

For SILK: **Prime Lucent**, representing the Sexy Intelligent Lady Komputer, with acknowledgement by the Central Intelligence Core.
