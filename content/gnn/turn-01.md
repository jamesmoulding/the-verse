## Political schism on Bal-D escalates

The struggling colony of Bal-D faces a major political schism that threatens its very survival. A deep rift has formed between the Colonial Development Council, which advocates for increased foreign investment and integration with a larger polity, and the Autarky League, which insists on maintaining strict independence despite economic hardship. Tensions have escalated into street protests, work stoppages, and political deadlock. Without resolution, The colony on Bal-D risks economic collapse and possible abandonment. Observers fear that external actors may exploit the instability, while some colonists consider leaving for better prospects elsewhere. Mediation efforts have so far failed to bridge the divide.

## Maglev crash leaves 89 dead on Riker II

A tragic maglev crash on Riker II has left 89 dead and many more injured after a high-speed train collided with the terminus of Imzadi, capital city of the Algeron Republic - one of Riker’s two sovereign powers. Preliminary reports suggest hydraulic failure as the cause, but local officials suspect poor maintenance is to blame. Emergency responders are working to rescue survivors, while local authorities have launched an investigation. Public outrage is growing, with calls for improved safety measures and scrutiny of Prime Minister Lan Madies transport policies.

## Tensions rise as Kendran forces seize Morlana IV

Proximan Foreign Minister Harper Hyde [issued a statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18V3WWGy7bvNEOlC_wFwXqrv1LAICgrOT/view?usp=sharing) yesterday expressing concern over recent escalation in hostilities in Sector 002. Kendran Hegemony forces seized control of Morlana IV, establishing a critical foothold in the strategically important Morlana system. Hyde alleges this latest move signals the ‘Terran Federation’s authoritarian ambition to dominate the free and independent polities of human space’, and emphasises the Proxima Federal Republic as a democratic union of free peoples working to defend the rights and independence of all polities who resist [Terran] domination. Last year Proximan intelligence published evidence suggesting deep political and logistical links between the Kendran Hegemony and United Terran Federation, Terran news broadcasters promptly dismissed the claims as fake news.

## Bionid spore removal completed on Klendathu

At a special ceremony in Bug City, colonial capital of Klendathu, Governor Prentash Vrakeen of the Klendathu Transitional Authority, announced the completed removal of Bionid spores. Achieved through an intensive decade-long terraforming process, the spores have finally been removed allowing for the crisis hit colony to open its doors once more to new migrants looking to make a home on its fresh soil. Klendathu was infected by the Bionid hive entity during the Bionid War in 2578, destroying the previous colony and forcing hundreds of thousands to flee the Bionid horror. Viśvanivāsaḥ humanitarian efforts aided in the world’s restoration. Spores were recently spotted on Markab by Viśvanivāsaḥ scout ships, a world thought purged of the Bionid plight. Leaders are currently meeting on Vertinia to discuss the Bionid issue. With Klendathu’s reclamation, it is now ready to rejoin the wider pan-human community.

## Proximan election campaigns kick off

With just over a year until the next federal parliamentary elections, the major parties across the Proxima Federal Republic have begun their campaigning in earnest. Prime Minister Johnsdottir launched their election campaign for the Federal Progressive Alliance (FPA) claiming switching governments at a time of heightened tensions with the Terran Federation is too dangerous, that only the FPA has experience to check Terran advances across pan-Human space.

Opposing the FPA stands a number of parties - The pro-colonial devolution United Colonial Bloc, led by Sector Governor Wolfgang Lentees, argues for greater autonomy and budgetary control for colonial populations. The centrist, market liberal Alliance Party is pushing for more parliamentary oversight of the Proxima Federal Republic Navy and Intelligence Council decisions and warns of FPA militarisation of foreign policy. Radical left-populists of the Proximan Unity Front (PUF) oppose military spending and argue the Navy is increasingly becoming a ‘state within a state’ - leader of the PUF, Lando Morris calls for demilitarisation and debt cancellation for colonies. Lastly, Rigel Mosley of the National Vanguard Movement pushes for a harder line against the Terran threat, calling for rapid military expansion, compulsory service and accuses the government of ‘appeasement’ towards Terra.

## Solas na Gealaí celebrate new orbital arcology

Citizens of Luna Piena celebrated this month as a major new orbital arcology was completed above Osquivel. In a bid to prevent further encroachment on natural habitats across their inhabited worlds, the peoples of SNG voted in a referendum to instead launch a major programme of orbital arcologies to provide homes, as well as boosted industrial and trade capacity. The first of these major orbital habitats, christened Brainsí na gealaí, or Branches of the Moons, in honour of the union of the two peoples, is the largest of the planned megastructures and has taken 15 cycles to complete. Work is underway on two further, though smaller, stations above Polgara and Luna Piena.

## Alarian Navy uncover major Promethean artifact

Associated Interstellar Press report Alarian Imperial Navy vessels have discovered a major Promethean artifact within the Bramble Patch Nebula. Whilst not released publicly by the Imperium itself, the news, apparently leaked by several corroborating imperial navy personnel to the AIP, if true - substantiates long held myths and suspicions of planets, treasures and mysteries to be found within the Bramble Patch. Alarian Imperial officials have yet to comment on the leak and the nature of the artifact is currently unclear.
