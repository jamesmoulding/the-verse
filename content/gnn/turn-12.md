## CEASEFIRE REACHED BETWEEN LGR-SKARO PACT

At the 11th hour, a [ceasefire has been agreed](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bvc_uTYxDDlbkm82nB7baDxBcDr1yR7tBdZwGz55ASA/edit?usp=sharing) between the Skaro Pact and Le Grande Republique. The parties agree to immediately cease offensive military operations, the Skaro Pact Organisations will immediately withdraw military forces from the NW Scouting Sector, the parties agree to recognise the independence of the people and colonies of Riker II. The Skaro Pact will release any and all prisoners of war and relinquish claims to LGR and independent worlds.

The Seekers Commonwealth released the following statement in response to the news of the ceasefire: “war amongst humanity makes us all poorer. The Seekers rejoice at the prospect of peace and urge all parties to strive for harmony.” SILK shared the following statement on their Ziv and Glimmr holochannels: “We are happy that peace is returning to the sector and congratulate all sides on the hard work taken to secure the deal.”

The UTF shared the following statement with GNN at the news: “The UTF welcomes the ceasefire achieved by the tireless work of our armed forces and our diplomats and our allies in the ASE. We are proud to have upheld the principles of planetary sovereignty and self determination despite its cost. We welcome now a new era of peace in the sector. And look forward to a bright harmonious future.”

## FELGOR FALLOUT: HOMEWORLDS BRACE FOR ESCALATION

News of the Proximan raid on Felgor has reached the homeworlds of Sector 001, and the response, arriving on Sector 005 comms relays only this cycle after the long delay from the Core, suggests the cold war between Proxima and Terra is closer a major escalation than at any point in living memory. Speaking in the Hall of the Republic, Prime Minister Svetlana Johnsdottir stood firmly behind the Republic’s governing authority in the sector, declaring that “Sector Governor Oskarsson acted with the full confidence of this government,” describing the operation as a calibrated act to prevent further atrocities by the Skaro Pact.

The Federation Chancellery on Earth has responded to the attack on their Sector 005 colonial capital in the strongest possible terms. Supreme Chancellor Mohammed El-Fridi condemned the raid as “a naked act of Proximan aggression against Terran citizens and the Terran way of life,” warning that the Federation’s response would be “serious”. Members of the Federal Council have gone further with several calling openly for retaliation against Proximan colonial holdings.

Within hours of the Chancellor’s address the United Terran Federation placed its armed forces on a footing of maximum readiness. Federal Navy battle fleets were observed breaking orbit and burning hard, their positions now unknown. GNN sources believe the fleets are holding under silent running at undisclosed jump off points across the sector. Proxima responded in kind, with PFRN battle fleets slipping their anchorages and vanishing from public tracking within hours of the Terran escalation. Ground forces on both sides have cancelled all leave, GNN understands, and are now on standby at undisclosed military staging areas. GNN defence correspondent Lira Vassenka said: “This is a very serious situation. With fleets sitting dark at hidden locations across Sector 001 and beyond, as the orders filter through, one miscalculation can trigger an escalation spiral to war”.

Diplomatic lines are hardening as Qaliph Hawiya Gentlos IV, Queeg Ambassador to the PACT Council, remarked the Queeg Heptarchy, still bristling at Terran accusations over Hobus two years ago, “will stand shoulder to shoulder with Proxima in whatever trials befall us”. Lord President Untar Fentzeen of the Union of Prax pledged that Prax would honour its obligations under Article V should the Federation strike at Proximan territory. The Court of Empress Alaria VI on Alioth offered a more guarded message, affirming the Imperium’s solidarity with its Skaro Pact partners in Sector 005 while pointedly declining to say whether the wider Imperium would follow the Federation into a war in the Core, its own Sector 005 squadrons still reeling from losses over Amity Station and Gloriana. The Vertinian Archon called on both powers to step back from the brink and enter into dialogue. Markets fell sharply on the news as reports arrived from Sector 005 and shipping insurers suspended cover on several routes. On outlying colonies across the Colonial Sectors, citizens of both powers fear they will bear the brunt of any war between the great powers. Stay tuned for more on this urgent story as it develops.

## SKARO FALLS TO FREE COLONIES FORCES

The Association of Free Colonies have announced they now ‘effectively control Skaro’ with large numbers of Alarian officials, scientists and technical staff now detained. GNN understands Free Colonies’ 2nd Volunteer Corps, working with allied special forces elements, have landed ground forces on Skaro, seizing the Alarian imperial proconsular offices and newly established government district. With the Alarian garrison recently withdrawn to fight in the LGR-Skaro Pact war, what limited skirmishes occurred between local Alarian security personnel and AFCO troops were brief with limited casualties.

At a press briefing yesterday, Free Colonies officials report their forces managed to prevent a major ‘terrorist false flag event’ undertaken by stay-behind Alarian Custodian units, who were neutralised before they could fully execute their plans, reports General Michael King of Joint Task Force ‘Spinner’. Despite their actions, more than 15 civilians are reported dead with many more injured following an apparent attempt by Alarian Custodian units to gun down civilians outside of an evacuation shelter, GNN understands.

Above Skaro an armada of Proximan, Vertinian and Visvanivasan naval vessels acted in support of the AFCO 2nd Volunteer Corps’ landing operations. Alarian state officials have condemned the assault and the involved parties as the ‘undoubted aggressors’ and the seizure of Skaro by Unity 5 forces as an act of ‘naked empire building’.

## NEW STAR SOCIALIST FEDERATION TO HOLD PEACE TALKS ON MOLLARI

Following a string of decisive victories by Deep Red militants on Mollari, the New Star Socialist Federation dispatched Admiral Kamin La Barre from Sector 001 to oversee peace talks on the colony. Since Deep Red militants led by leader Katya Posada launched their campaign against the Mollari Republic last year several key colony settlements within the Mollari Republic have fallen to the insurgent faction. In recent months DeepCore, a major mining conglomerate and subsidiary of Atlas Stellar Extraction, have ceased operations on the colony over spirally costs and security concerns, lodging a formal protest with Mollari Republic leaders.

Local sources speaking to GNN last week report of new efforts by the rebels to consolidate their gains in the equatorial rainforest belt, with new training camps and base areas established deep inside the jungle. Deep Red ranks have swelled in recent months as exploited local workers and DeepCore contractors fled from their postings to join the rebels in the jungle. Volunteer fighters from neighbouring Trotsky have also made the journey in their thousands, looking to join the prospective revolution on the planet - sparking Mollari officials to clamp down on trade and travel from the neighbouring colony. Mollari Republic officials have so far refused to comment on the situation, claiming the issue simply as a matter of internal security. GNN spoke with a Mollari senior figure on condition of anonymity, who said “the situation could not be worse, this threatens to become a full blown rebellion and the very future of our colony is now at stake”.

Working conditions in the DeepCore ‘pits’ as they are known locally, have long been the subject of intermittent complaints by locals, lower level colonial officials and journalists alike as Mollari leaders have sought to capitalise on and expand DeepCore’s investment in the colony through providing the recent Free Enterprise Regulation - stripping the colonial charter of basic workers’ rights and protections and allowing for the establishment of company towns and harsh work regimes across the colony. With DeepCore’s mines closed down and revenue streams closed off, analysts expect financial belt-tightening to take place calling for strict financial measures and economic measures across the colony, which is expected to only add further fuel to the fire, they say.

Little is known about the intentions of the New Star Socialist Federation and their envoy Admiral Kamin La Barre, who is due to meet with Mollari Republic officials this cycle. La Barre is a renowned diplomat, strategist and part-time archeologist, formerly serving aboard the CASI flagship CSS Polis, GNN’s Kara Stuart notes. The NSSF have also announced the development of formal diplomatic ties with neighbouring Trotsky, with Lars Makhno, General Secretary of the Red Planet Initiative Central Committee, speaking to GNN, New Pravda and the Daily Worker, hailing ties with “our comrades across the stars”. Alarian broadcast journalist Miser Juril told viewers: “We don’t agree with the Federation on economics. Never will. But we agree on one thing. Republicans and their ilk are a menace to every decent state in this sector, and it’s time somebody besides us said so. With warships.” La Barre himself was unavailable for comment when approached by GNN, he is currently commanding the NSSF Third Battle Squadron, orbiting Mollari.

Neighbouring Basha Colony was once more unavailable for comment, the southern continent farming colonies being partway through their winter celebrations. Reginald Bivvy, Lord Mayor of Basha Colony, did offer a short remark to GNN between engagements, calling for "both sides to end this unfortunate business, lay down their tools and join us in a warm glass of Malak, after which these disagreements will surely look much less important".

## UNUSUAL ACTIVITY SPOTTED OVER MORBIS - COLONIAL LEADERS REQUEST POWERS RESPECT SOVEREIGNTY

GNN has received reports from colonists and trading vessels of the Morbis colony world, citing large fleet movements over their moon. Speaking to GNN, Karush Lemoon of the merchant freighter *Old Sally* says “I’m telling you, I saw them. There’s a Terran Battle Squadron just sitting out there. A whole bloody fleet. What on Old Earth they think they’re doing I have no idea, but I’m telling you, something’s up - maybe it’s the haunted moon. Either way, we don’t want no war round here, not good for business.”

A small colony world, Morbis was settled by the Imperial Colonisation Bureau over one hundred and fifty years ago, and is best known for being largely uninhabitable due to a particularly aggressive hexapodal predator known as a skirver. As a result, despite its lush biome and rich ecosystem, perfect for broader colonial expansion by any account, the existing colonial settlements are focused on a broad high steppe plateau covering a swathe of the planet’s northern continent - far out of the reach of the migratory packs of skirvers.

Salem Jacket, leader of one of the planet’s three colonial groupings, remarked “we’ve been relatively isolated these past few years, but since the interstellar comms back online recently we’ve seen a real growth of interest on the colony and the products we produce, from skirver leather to javanite crystals. The High Falls Cooperative welcomes any and all to come and visit, but - and I think speak for Fornem and the other colonies, when I say, we’d ask the major powers to respect our territorial sovereignty and leave us well alone.”

## REASON CITIZENS PULL TOGETHER TO REBUILD

News of Le Grande Republique’s rebuilding efforts have taken the holonet by storm across the sector this cycle with footage from its battered capital, showing citizen volunteers clearing rubble, running field kitchens and guarding makeshift shelters in the districts around Place Charles de Gaulle. The campaign, which GNN understands was coordinated at the highest levels of the LGR government, presents Reason as a colony that refused to break despite heavy bombardment and kowtow to life “under the Alarian yoke”. In a recorded holobroadcast, Consul Georges DanTron said: “They came to Reason expecting a surrender. They found a people united. We were alone once, and we paid dearly for it. But look around us now, here - and to our friends across the sector. We are not alone now.”

Lucile Desmoulins.4RR, acting prefect of the Reason government district, told GNN that volunteer brigades had restored water to several neighbourhoods without government help. “Nobody ordered them to do this. Every brick lifted from those streets is an answer to everyone who said the Republique would not last the year. This is citizenship in action - democracy in action. It will be a long road, but we will rebuild anew, and stronger than ever, together.”

Aid workers on the ground paint a more sober picture. Dr Ilse Marchetti, field coordinator for Médecins Sans Frontières on Reason, described the volunteer effort as real and remarkable, but warned it could not replace sustained international relief. “I have watched teenagers dig the deceased and dessicated bodies of a family out of a collapsed apartment block with their bare hands,” she said. “Courage does not set a fracture or help to purify water. Our clinics are still treating severe burn and crush injuries months since the attacks, and we are now seeing the first cases of waterborne disease, and we are rationing anaesthetic. What Reason needs is not more footage. It is safe access, medical supplies and power.” One relief worker, speaking on condition of anonymity, put it more bluntly: “It is true enough that people are optimistic for the future - the government here has done very well in the circumstances, but there are still many with not enough to eat.”

Where Amity Station and Unison have received significant humanitarian aid from SILK and the Visvanivasah Confederacy, the Cerean Planetary Alliance and the Vertinian Commonwealth - including Vertinian environmental engineers and scientists, very little international support has reached the capital of the LGR, with the humanitarian situation, as Dr Marchetti noted, still very precarious.

## FIRST CHILD SUBJECT OF LATEST UNDERGROUND HIT

Music is in the air across Sector 005 this cycle, as a smash new hit is heard in nightclubs across the Verse. Known for its underground nightclub scene, [Big Man Little Boy](https://suno.com/song/d449840a-8d84-4c7d-8c53-44b22af059ec) has been heard across on Gloriana, Sanctuaria, Sacramentis - and in more legitimate nightclubs beyond the Empire, from the LGR to the VCW. The smash hit has outpaced even Mystiq’s latest track which launched at Crashdump’s XENU Arena last week.

Leá Everdore of holomag ENZK, one of the sector’s foremost music blogs, says Big Man Little Boy has now been remixed a dozen times. Everyone knows what this is all about, she says, like the joke goes “A Free Colonist and a Visvani walk into Ashborn’s bar - Ashborn asks what they want, but they just reach over the bar and take what they want,” she chuckles to herself before remarking “we haven’t seen something like this LO0ZY’s early stuff, a proper viral phenomenon”.

The First Child left Sector 005 this cycle on a mission to Sector 001 to see the Empress in person, the Imperial Broadcast Service reports. He is unlikely to return until late 2603 given the travel times involved. In his stead, his deputy, now Acting Governor Bastion Kent has been elevated. Bastion is a veteran of the Bionid campaigns of the 2580s, was later appointed to the Imperial Court and has served as Chief Advisor and Minister to the Alarian colonial government in Sector 002, and more recently, Sector 005. Rumours are circulating that this may be a permanent appointment, with senior sources across the sector suggestion he is being recalled by the Empress to be replaced, given his strategic failures, a raid on the Alarian colonial capital and the loss of Skaro in the recent LGR-Skaro Pact conflict. Senior SNG officials remarked: “It is not unexpected for one to be called into a 121 with your line manager in the event of a serious fault, such as the recent loss of military assets at Gloriana.”

## OUTLYING WORLDS HIT BY PIRATE ATTACKS

Pirate raids have increased significantly in the last cycle, GNN understands. Freighters arriving at and leaving outlying colonies report being stopped near the M25 limit and boarded by armed crews. Some had part of their cargo taken. Others were simply harassed before being turned back at cannon point. No known vessels have been reported lost and only a handful of casualties have been reported. Incidents have occured on Neo Sendai, Aeryn, Snog, Meero, Bix, O’Neill and Mankey - K10 and Dross have faced particular issue. Convoys with hired private escorts appear to have been left alone. The raiders have gone after lone traders and the smaller, more remote independent colonies instead.

Professor Y’ah Mee-Hartese, Director of the SaferStars Initiative, told GNN he was “very concerned” by the reports. “What worries me is not the size of any one incident, it is how widespread they are. These crews are clearly disciplined, they are choosing easy targets and they are clearly avoiding a fight. It appears they are using smaller spotter craft to trail freighters from station to the M25 limit, how else could they find such ships out there in the deep black?” he said. Mee-Hartese warned that independent worlds with no naval protection, and no money for escorts, are now significantly exposed. He added that insurers and hauliers may start pricing the outer colonies out of regular trade altogether and expects goods shortages on several routes.

Reports suggest SILK forces have been actively engaging in deterring pirate raids in recent months and are engaged in attempts to locate the pirate holdouts, rumours suggest. To better bolster these efforts and improve SILK’s capacities for anti-piracy operations, SILK’s 5/1st Fleet and ground forces carried out Operation Trapped Sparrow practicing pirate interception, detection and capture.
