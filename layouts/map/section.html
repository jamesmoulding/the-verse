Unit movement

    Key

      Drag, or use WASD or the arrow keys, to pan. Cmd-drag (Mac) or Alt-drag (Windows) to rotate, or right-drag. Scroll or pinch to zoom where you point. Hover over a system for a summary. Click it to zoom in and open its details, then click it again or Enter Star System to visit its colony.

      Distances

      20 movement points a turn (100 days). 1 MP per hex (5 days); entering or leaving a system costs its MP. Messages: 2.5 days per hex.
